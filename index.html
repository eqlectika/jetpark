Mint & Hack

Touch Controls: Drag up or down on the left half of the screen for throttle; drag on the right half to tilt.

Desktop Controls: Use Arrow keys (← ↑ ↓ →) or hold left mouse button and drag to tilt; hold Space for forward thrust (double-tap Space for boost) or B for reverse thrust.

Auto-Hack: Rings and nearby players inside your aim cone are collected automatically when in range.

Build Banks: Drag the crosshair to reposition. Long-press it to build a bank at screen center (1 token per tick, 1 token fee to author). Tap it to switch mode: Sky / Flat / Cube.

Free Flight & Gravity: Fly in any direction indefinitely. Gravity is zero at the planet center and zero at the matching outer height.
